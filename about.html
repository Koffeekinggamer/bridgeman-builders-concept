Studio

A builder’s practice, not a production line.

Bridgeman Builders is Corey Bridgeman — a principal-led studio in Landrum, building custom residences for the Carolina highlands and the Upstate.

Principal

Corey Bridgeman

Clients in this region have no shortage of companies that will build them a house. What they rarely find is a builder who will still be on the decking the week the stone arrives — and who will still answer two years after handover.

Corey founded the studio to work at that scale. Few projects. Direct conversation. Trades chosen for the way they finish a corner, not the way they fill a calendar.

The office is in Landrum, at the foot of the escarpment, where South Carolina leans into North Carolina. That geography is not branding. It is the work: ridgeline lots, hardwood canopy, rock, weather, and views that should not be wasted on a house that could sit anywhere.

Beliefs

What we will not trade away.

The site is the brief.

A house copied from another lot is already a compromise. We walk the land before we talk square footage.

Luxury is a standard, not a word.

Joinery that closes. Stone that is laid, not applied. Assemblies that hold in a Carolina summer and a mountain winter. That is the work. The word can stay off the façade.

Clarity is a form of craft.

Budgets, allowances, and schedules are spoken plainly. A client should never have to decode their own project.

Where we work

Upstate South Carolina and Western North Carolina.

Landrum and the Tryon foothills. Greenville’s ridgelines. Hendersonville and Flat Rock. Asheville and the highlands. Lake sites when the land is right.

If the project is farther, we will say so. Distance is a quality issue before it is a logistics issue.
